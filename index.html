Meet the founders

Nobody is making TV for families anymore. So we did.

After 17 years inside Hollywood, husband-and-wife team Curtis and Cara Leopardo saw what most people in the industry won't say out loud: the content has gotten worse, the standards have collapsed, and families have quietly accepted shows they have to apologize for, mute, or watch alone.

We The Studios is the alternative. We produce and stream original reality series with the production value of a major network — and a hard line on what doesn't belong in family viewing: no profanity, no sexual innuendo, no glorified drinking, no agenda baked into the storyline. Not "lightly edited." Not "mostly clean." Actually clean.

New episodes drop weekly. A subscription is about the price of a coffee. And every show is one you can actually put on with your kids in the room — not because you've checked a rating, but because we built it that way from the first frame.
