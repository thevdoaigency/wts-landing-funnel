We The Studios — Funnel Architecture

Three-stage conversion flow from awareness to subscription choice.

Stage 1

Awareness — IG / YouTube

Owned social channels + paid placement

• 15–30 second teaser clips
• Hook viewers with real family moments
• Link in bio / description → landing page
• No friction. One click to stage 2.
Teaser Videos
15–30 sec clips from the full trailer, posted to IG feed, IG Reels, YouTube shorts, and paid social placements
↓
Stage 2

Consideration — Landing Page

Hero trailer + single conversion moment

• Full 45–60 second trailer plays here
• "Come home to what matters" positioning
• One clear CTA: "Start watching now"
• Pricing hint in small print only: "$3.98"
• Trust sections below (testimonials, founders, features)
Landing Page
Hero section with full trailer, headline, and single "Start watching now" button. Below: testimonials, show grid, and social proof.
↓
Stage 3

Decision — Pricing Page

Plan selection with clear value differentiation

• User clicks "Start watching now" → lands here
• Now they see the full choice (annual vs. monthly)
• Annual highlighted as best value
• Both options: full access to all shows
• Clear: No hidden fees. Cancel anytime.

Core Principle

Stage 2 removes friction (one button, minimal pricing info). Stage 3 presents the choice. The journey: Teaser → Full experience → Commit. Never show pricing on stage 2. Never show the trailer on stage 3. Clean separation, clear intent at each step.
