Raising Reynolds: Season Two - Adoption, Family, and Mule Magic

Welcome back to another incredible season of Raising Reynolds! This season brings more laughter, chaos, and heartwarming moments from Blake and Emily Reynolds as they continue their journey raising four amazing adopted children: Brianna (15), Mikayla (13), Kingston (10), and Allie (4).

A Season of Firsts

Season 2 opens with a household in full swing—and Blake has some new challenges to tackle. The season kicks off with the family building a chicken coop together, a project that quickly becomes more than just construction—it's a lesson in responsibility, teamwork, and the importance of giving back.

Major Moments from Season 2

🐔 Chicken Coop Construction

The Reynolds decide it's time for an upgrade to their chicken coop after realizing their old one wasn't cut out for their growing kids—and their active chickens. Blake enlists the help of Brianna, Mikayla, Kingston, and Allie in what becomes a full-day family project. What starts as a practical solution turns into a heart-to-heart conversation about race, parenting mixed-race children in Kentucky, and teaching principles over perfection.

🌲 The Treasure Hunt Adventure

A mysterious neighbor named Anton Panda shows up with a treasure map, promising adventure in the woods. What begins as skepticism from Emily quickly transforms into pure magic as the family embarks on an unforgettable journey through the forest. This episode includes one of the most emotional moments of the season: Blake using wolf howls to call for their missing son Kingston, leading to a tear-jerking reunion and reflection on family bonds.

🎢 Fun Spot Orlando Adventure

The Reynolds take the kids to Fun Spot America in Orlando—and what follows is pure entertainment gold. From the first roller coaster (complete with Emily's terrifying scream) to feeding alligators face-to-face, this episode showcases Blake's fearless dad persona trying to maintain his cool while secretly terrified of heights, snakes, and gators.

  • The Freedom Fighter roller coaster ride
  • Feeding gators at the reptile exhibit
  • Kingston's first time on a go-kart (with a win for Dad!)
  • Emily's panic over snake handling while the kids beg to touch everything

🛠️ Heavy Machinery Mayhem

Kingston gets his "rite of passage"—driving an excavator to install a flagpole in preparation for a family party. What starts as a teaching moment quickly turns chaotic when Kingston accidentally destroys Emily's beloved mini-car (affectionately nicknamed "Black Betty"). The fallout leads to one of the funniest scenes in the season: Blake decides to smash the car with the very same excavator, complete with Donald Trump surviving the crash and the family celebrating the destruction.

🏠 Role Reversal Day

In a brilliant twist, Blake and Emily switch roles for a day—the kids run the household while Mom and Dad chill on the patio. What follows is chaos, creative cooking attempts (including pickles smoothies), and the ultimate test of parenting skills when the kids realize just how much work goes into running a household.

🐎 Mule Magic

Allie's dream of having a horse comes true—but not quite as expected. What arrives isn't a horse but a beautiful, gentle 20-year-old mule named Molly (or "Molly the Mule"). The season finale features Allie riding her new companion while Blake tries to keep up with the family's expanding animal kingdom.

Themes of Season 2
  • Adoption & Identity: The Reynolds reflect on their adoption journey and the importance of celebrating each child's unique background
  • Parenting Mixed-Race Children: Blake shares his honest thoughts on raising half-Black children in Kentucky without assuming cultural knowledge he doesn't possess
  • Family Traditions: From adoption day parties to flagpole installations, the family creates new traditions while honoring old ones
  • Health & Wellness: Emily pushes for healthier living with workouts and juicing—Blake reluctantly participates with signature humor

"We learned early on that we were not going to be able to have children. All of our friends who got married at the same time as us were having kids after kids, after kids. We ended up saying, okay, Lord, will do foster care, but we only want two kids. We ended up taking three at one time."

The season closes with Blake reflecting on their journey and encouraging others to consider fostering or adoption:

"There are so many kids in our system today that are in foster care and need of either adopting or a foster home... I encourage everybody to do the foster care process. Do the adoption process. There's kids out there that don't have somebody to call dad."

What's Next?

With their animal family expanding, their kids thriving, and their home filled with laughter (and occasional chaos), Season 2 of Raising Reynolds proves once again why this family resonates with audiences worldwide.

The main lesson? Family isn't about biology—it's about love, commitment, and being there for each other through every chicken coop build, treasure hunt, and mule adventure.
